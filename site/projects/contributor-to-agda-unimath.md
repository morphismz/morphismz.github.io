I have contributed nearly 10K lines of machine checkable, formalized programs and proofs and made 56 commits as part of a large-scale, open source formal verification project. I have formalized parts of my honors thesis, helped to refactor code, and formalized important higher coherences arising in Homotopy Type Theory.

### Parts of my thesis that I have formalized

#### The Eckmann-Hilton argument

I contributed the "homotopical version" of the Eckmann-Hilton argument to agda-unimath. This version of the argument allows one to compute the transport along an Eckmann-Hilton identification. See the [Eckmann-Hilton](https://unimath.github.io/agda-unimath/synthetic-homotopy-theory.eckmann-hilton-argument.html) page for both the argument and the computation of transport.

#### The universal property of the family of fibers of a map.

I contributed a formalization of the unversal property of the family of fibers of a map, found on [this page](https://unimath.github.io/agda-unimath/foundation.universal-property-family-of-fibers-of-maps.html#properties).
