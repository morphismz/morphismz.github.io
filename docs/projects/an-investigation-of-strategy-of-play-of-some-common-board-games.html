An investigation of strategy of play of some common board games

An ongoing project investigating the mathematical properties of common board games, aiming to provide optimal strategies of play. Board games considered include Catan, Azul, and more. Using tools from probability theory and game theory, the goal of this project is to provide heuristics for better play and spell out an optimal strategy of play.
