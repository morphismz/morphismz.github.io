Raymond Baker's Personal (World Wide) Website

My name is Raymond Baker. I am currently working as a Haskell programmer at MasterWord Services building business management infrastructure. Previously I worked as a high school math teacher at Temple Grandin School, a small school serving students on autism the spectrum. And before that I studied mathematics and philosophy at CU Boulder. I graduated in 2023 with with the honors summa cum laude. And before that I spent a number of years as a child.

I enjoy doing mathematics, especially math in the realm of topology and geometry. As a student, I wrote an honors thesis titled "Eckmann-Hiltion and the Hopf Fibration in Homotopy Type Theory". I presented this thesis at a few math conferences and formalized a few parts of the thesis in agda-unimath. See the talks page for more details about the former, and the relevant projects page for more details about the latter.
